## 🎯 The 3 Core Modules Powering DANGER.LABS

### 1. Meta Social & Engagement Suite
* **Meta Graph API Pipeline:** Direct publishing and scheduling to Instagram Professional and Facebook Pages.
* **Agency Caption Engine:** Multi-style AI copywriting (Hook-Story-Offer, Minimal Punchy, Carousel Breakdown, Event Promo).
* **Live Chatbot:** Auto-replies to comments matching DANGER.LABS or specific client brand guidelines.
* **Lead-Magnet Trigger:** Automatic comment-to-DM funnels (*"Comment 'DANGER' for our exclusive client package"*).

### 2. Agency Operations & CRM Hub
* **Multi-Client Brand Profiles:** Switch between DANGER.LABS and client workspaces. Each brand holds its own tone, rules, and Meta keys.
* **Deliverables Board:** Track ongoing Website Dev, UI/UX designs, CRM software builds, and upcoming shoots.
* **Client Approval Portal:** Send clients a single clean link to approve their upcoming week of content, design proofs, or shoot schedules with one click.

### 3. Inbound Funnel & Task Automations
* **Lead Capture:** Automatically collect inquiries from comments, website forms, and event ads into an actionable lead list.
* **Email Staging:** Draft and sync lead lists to client email marketing tools.
* **Task Automations:** Low-friction scripts to automate repetitive daily tasks (asset resizing, file naming, weekly client performance summaries).
