# Posting voice

- Keep drafts short, clear, useful, and confident.
- Use light wit or sarcasm about industry habits. Never mock the audience, customers, or our product.
- X drafts: at most 280 characters, two lines, and two hashtags.
- Live drafts use a relevant feed item, a company-workspace angle, and its source link. No invented facts.
- If feeds have no matching item, use an evergreen draft; do not call it breaking news.
- Review every draft before publishing.

The **Random post** button uses these rules. Live sources: Hacker News, Lobste.rs, and Dev.to.
Draft topics and source links are recorded per company in SQLite; repeated and near-matching topics are skipped. 👍/👎 shifts future template selection. If every available topic was used, the button asks for a new trend or topic instead of repeating one.
